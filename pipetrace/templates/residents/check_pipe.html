{% translate "You need:" %} {% translate "a coin or a key" %} {% translate "a fridge magnet" %} {% translate "your phone camera" %}

  1. {% translate "Find where the water pipe comes in" %}

    {% translate "Look in the basement, crawl space, garage or utility closet, usually on the wall that faces the street, near your water meter or main shut-off valve. In warmer places the meter may be outside in a box near the curb; check the pipe that goes into the house." %}

    {% translate "Check the stretch of pipe between the wall or floor and the shut-off valve or meter." %}

  2. {% translate "Scratch it gently with the coin" %}

    {% translate "Pick a spot on the pipe and scrape the edge of the coin across it a few times, like a lottery ticket. If the pipe is painted, scratch through the paint. Don't use a knife or a file." %}

    {% translate "Lead is soft: the scratch turns bright, shiny silver." %}

  3. {% translate "Hold a magnet against it" %}

    {% translate "A fridge magnet sticks to galvanized steel. It does not stick to lead, copper or plastic." %}

  4. {% translate "Take a close-up photo and send it to us" %}

    {% translate "Photograph the scratched spot with the pipe going into the wall or floor in the picture. We'll look at it and update our records." %}

    {% translate "Send us your photo" %}
